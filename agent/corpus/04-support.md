# Halden Systems — Support Tiers

Support is sold separately from hardware and software.

**Bronze** support costs 1,200 euro per year. Response time is two business days.
It covers email only.

**Silver** support costs 3,600 euro per year. Response time is four business hours.
It covers email and telephone.

**Gold** support costs 9,000 euro per year. Response time is one business hour.
It covers email, telephone and on-site attendance within 48 hours.

Support contracts run for twelve months from the purchase date and cannot be cancelled
part way through the term.
