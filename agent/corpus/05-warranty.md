# Halden Systems — Warranty Policy

All Halden Systems hardware carries a 24-month warranty from the date of shipment.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover water ingress, damage from
installation outside the documented mounting tolerances, or any unit whose enclosure has
been opened by a party other than Halden Systems.

Warranty replacements ship within five business days of the returned unit being received.

An extended warranty adding a further 24 months is available for 18 percent of the unit
list price, and must be purchased within 90 days of the original shipment date.
