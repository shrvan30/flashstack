# Halden Systems — Data and Security Policy

Sensor telemetry is stored in the customer's own infrastructure. Halden Systems does not
host customer measurement data.

The Halden Insight platform retains diagnostic logs for 90 days, after which they are
deleted automatically.

Halden Systems holds ISO 27001 certification, first awarded in July 2023.
The company underwent its most recent third-party penetration test in January 2026.

Customer data is never used for training or benchmarking without a signed data-sharing
addendum.
