# Halden Systems — Volume Discounts

Volume discounts apply to hardware only. They do not apply to licences or support.

Orders of 10 to 24 units of a single product receive a 5 percent discount.
Orders of 25 to 49 units of a single product receive a 12 percent discount.
Orders of 50 or more units of a single product receive a 20 percent discount.

Discounts are calculated per product line, not across the whole order.
Volume discounts cannot be combined with any promotional pricing.
