# Halden Systems — Company Overview

Halden Systems is a private industrial sensor company founded in 2011 in Tromso, Norway.
It employs 214 people across three offices: Tromso (headquarters, 130 staff),
Gdansk (engineering, 61 staff) and Halifax (sales and support, 23 staff).

The chief executive is Marit Osland, appointed in March 2019.
The company's fiscal year ends on 31 March.
Revenue for fiscal year 2025 was 48.6 million euro.

Halden Systems sells only to industrial customers; there is no consumer product line.
