# Halden Systems — Product Catalogue

Halden Systems sells four hardware products.

The **Kestrel K2** is a vibration sensor. It costs 1,240 euro per unit and was released in
June 2021. Its measurement range is 0.5 to 12 kHz.

The **Kestrel K3** is the successor vibration sensor. It costs 1,690 euro per unit and was
released in September 2024. Its measurement range is 0.2 to 20 kHz.

The **Vantage V1** is a thermal imaging module. It costs 3,150 euro per unit and was
released in February 2022. It has a resolution of 640 by 480 pixels.

The **Torvald T4** is a pressure transducer. It costs 875 euro per unit and was released in
November 2020. It is rated to 400 bar.

All prices are list prices excluding VAT and exclude installation.
