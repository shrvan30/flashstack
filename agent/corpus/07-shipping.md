# Halden Systems — Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 Central European Time ship the same business day.

Standard shipping within the European Union takes three business days and costs 45 euro
per order regardless of quantity.
Express shipping within the European Union takes one business day and costs 130 euro.
Shipping to North America takes seven business days and costs 310 euro.

Orders with a hardware subtotal above 20,000 euro ship free of charge by the standard
service.

Halden Systems does not ship to addresses outside the European Union and North America.
