# Halden Systems — Calibration

Every sensor ships with a calibration certificate valid for 12 months.

Recalibration is required annually to keep the measurement accuracy specification valid.
Recalibration at a Halden Systems facility costs 210 euro per unit and takes 10 business days.
On-site recalibration costs 640 euro per visit plus 95 euro per unit and is available only
to customers holding Gold support.

A sensor that is more than 24 months past its last calibration date cannot be recalibrated
and must be replaced.
