# Halden Systems — Returns and Refunds

Unopened hardware may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Opened hardware may be returned within 30 days subject to a restocking fee of 15 percent
of the list price. After 30 days no returns are accepted except under warranty.

Software licences are non-refundable once activated.
Support contracts are non-refundable.

All returns require a Return Material Authorisation number issued by the Halifax office.
