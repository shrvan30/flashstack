# Halden Systems — Software Licensing

The Halden Insight analytics platform is licensed per site, not per user or per sensor.

A **Standard** site licence costs 4,800 euro per year and covers up to 50 connected sensors.
A **Extended** site licence costs 11,500 euro per year and covers up to 250 connected sensors.
An **Unlimited** site licence costs 27,000 euro per year with no sensor cap.

Licences renew annually on the anniversary of activation.
A licence downgrade takes effect only at the next renewal date.
There is no perpetual licence option.
